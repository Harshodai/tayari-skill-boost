## Strategic Framework & Campaign Architecture

### 1. The Market Problem in 2026
Engineering bootcamps and career accelerators face intense placement rate pressure. Simultaneously, generic "AI auto-apply" bots and generative resume tools produce hallucinated skills, keyword-stuffed PDFs, and fabricated achievements that get alumni blacklisted by corporate ATS systems and hiring managers.

### 2. The Tayari Differentiation
- **Zero-Hallucination Guarantee**: Every bullet point and claim is validated against the candidate's verified claim ledger.
- **Calibrated Fit Engine**: Replaces blind keyword stuffing with semantic gap analysis and evidence-based matching.
- **Auditable Submission Receipts**: Provides career coaches with transparent logs of where and how alumni are applying.
- **Honest Free Scan Funnel**: Directors can test `/free-scan` immediately with zero signup friction or credit card barriers.

### 3. Sequence Structure
Every target organization below receives a 3-touch cadence:
- **Touch 1 (Day 1)**: *The ATS Hallucination Trap vs. Calibrated Proof* (Problem + Direct Solution + Honest Free Scan CTA)
- **Touch 2 (Day 4)**: *How Career Coaches Use the Claim Ledger* (Operational Workflow + Placement Stats + Coach Time Savings)
- **Touch 3 (Day 8)**: *10-Minute Cohort Pilot & Live Benchmark Scan* (Low-friction Pilot Offer + Direct Scheduling)
